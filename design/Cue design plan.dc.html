Cue — design plan

Internal task tracker for the HIJ media, worship and production team. Mobile first. Name: keeping Cue — it is the production word for the moment something has to happen, it is short enough for a top bar, and it does not read as SaaS. Greenroom and Callsheet both name a place or a document rather than a deadline.

02 Type

Archivo for the interface and IBM Plex Mono for dates, file sizes and timestamps: Archivo is a slightly narrow grotesque, so long song titles fit on a 360px row without truncating, and its large x-height holds up at 14px on a phone; Plex Mono has true tabular figures so due dates and file sizes line up in a column and can be scanned without reading.

03 The overdue treatment

No badge on the right. The row itself changes state: an overdue task becomes a solid oxblood band across the full width of the list, text reversed out in near-white, and the due date is replaced by how late it is — 4 days late, not Tue 12 Aug. Row height and layout are identical to a normal row, so nothing jumps; the list reads as a stack of quiet white rows with dark bars where the problems are, legible from across a room. Everything else in the product stays monochrome.

05 Checked against "do not do these"

Two things I changed after reviewing the plan against that list:

  • My first instinct was a 3px oxblood left border on overdue rows. That is the generic tell — a coloured accent stripe on a card. Replaced with the full reversed band.
  • I had a status pill in three colours. Cut: status is text, and the only colour in the list is the overdue band. Colour that means one thing is worth more than colour everywhere.
  • Icons will be a single outline set at 20px (Lucide, 1.5px stroke), stroke in ink-2. Logo slot is an outlined 28px box marked "logo" in the top bar — I am not inventing a mark.
